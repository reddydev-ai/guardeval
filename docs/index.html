Specific product decisions

A roadmap also requires saying no

The work deliberately prioritizes evidence traceability and controllable automation over a public chatbot demo or unverified model-quality claims. These are prototype scope choices, not verified decisions from a commercial PM organization.

No public backend yet

The Docker services could be hosted, but user identity, document permissions and safe ingestion are incomplete. Only static portfolio pages are exposed.

No autonomous final approvals

FlowAgent can prepare risk context, but vendor decisions require an explicit separate reviewer action. Missing documents enforce a deterministic minimum risk.

No quality badge from CI

Five retrieval and six agent scenarios confirm selected behavior; they cannot establish a calibrated answer-quality or hallucination rate.

Measure before promising ROI

Compare time to a correctly cited policy answer and successful onboarding completion with an observed manual baseline. No user study or realized savings is claimed.

Read the full release decision record and proposed user research plan.

What is not yet proven

Model quality: No paid, credentialed generative-model evaluation on an independently reviewed held-out dataset has been completed. The draft challenge cases are not scored benchmarks.

Production readiness: Per-user authorization, document ACLs, secure file sandboxing, realistic reliability SLOs, audit logging and broader adversarial validation remain open.

Business impact: No adoption, ROI, customer savings or employer deployment outcomes are attributed to these public repositories.
